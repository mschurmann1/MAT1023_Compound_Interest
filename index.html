How these are calculated

Monthly savings:

\[ PMT = \frac{\text{annual income} \times \text{savings rate}}{12} \]

Future value, where \(r\) is the annual return as a decimal, \(n = 12\) compounding periods per year, and \(t\) is the number of years:

\[ FV = PMT \cdot \frac{\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} - 1}{\frac{r}{n}} \]

Total contributed:

\[ \text{Total} = PMT \cdot nt \]

Interest earned:

\[ \text{Interest} = FV - \text{Total} \]
